These estimates assume an existing SOS token account and the treasury’s SOL token account; creating missing accounts and wallet priority fees can cost extra. Actual NFT sizes may vary.

Why the earlier estimate was 0.01955348 SOL +

The previous farm design combined the 0.01 SOL price, 0.004 SOL platform fees, 0.00001 SOL network fees, about 0.0031 SOL pass rent, and 0.0007366 SOL purchase-record rent. That was 0.0178466 SOL spent, plus a 0.00170688 SOL staking-seat deposit refundable on full withdrawal. Calling the whole 0.01955348 SOL permanent spending was imprecise.

The removed SOS reward seed was paid by the project. Removing it does not reduce the platform or NFT costs. This version replaces the refundable seat with a nontransferable deposit receipt, adding about 0.0031 SOL of NFT rent instead.

ⓘ

No staking rewards or reward pool. The only SOS vault holds player deposits. After seven days, each unredeemed receipt returns exactly 1,000 SOS once; there is no seed or emission for the first staker to claim. Operator trust is required: the project authority can move vault funds, issue or change receipts, and pause withdrawals. Withdrawals require enough SOS in the vault and an enabled recipe. A receipt does not guarantee that the vault remains fully backed.
